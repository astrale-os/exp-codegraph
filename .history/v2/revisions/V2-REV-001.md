# V2-REV-001: Keep rationale outside normative specification identity

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-08-13

Affected requirements: V2-SPC-004, V2-SPC-005, V2-QLF-011

Review perspectives: TypeSpec product, analysis/native, consumer/DX, qualification

## Existing decision

The V1 `module-v2` loader includes the rendered `architecture.md` revision in `specRevision` even
though architecture text is rationale and presentation context. Editing prose therefore changes the
identity used as if normative module behavior had changed.

## Proposed decision

`SpecificationSnapshot.id` and `revision` include only normalized authored normative resources and
static compilation diagnostics. `architecture.md`, `.history/`, icons, implementation bindings,
resolved tests, and filesystem observations retain independent revisions owned by their respective
context, analysis, qualification, or presentation projections.

## Evidence and motivation

The ratified ADR already separates specification, analysis, qualification, and presentation
authorities. The complete-corpus audit proves the new compiler preserves every authored field over
309 modules. The focused regression changes only `architecture.md` and proves the normative
snapshot identity remains unchanged while V1 would have changed `specRevision`.

## Alternatives

- Preserve the V1 digest behavior. Rejected because it gives rationale normative authority and
  causes unnecessary conformance and cache invalidation.
- Omit architecture from the product. Rejected because rationale remains useful context; it simply
  needs an independent context revision.
- Normalize architecture text into the normative snapshot. Rejected because normalization cannot
  turn non-normative prose into module behavior.

## Compatibility, migration, and gate effects

The difference is internal identity behavior for rationale-only edits. Authored contracts and
user-facing conformance do not change. Shadow parity records it as a defect correction. Gate 3 owns
the compiler regression; Gate 4 must not treat the expected identity difference as unexplained
semantic drift; presentation retains architecture through a separate projection before Gate 5.

## Objections and dispositions

- Product objection: an architecture edit should still refresh the viewer. Accepted; the context
  projection has its own revision and can refresh without invalidating normative qualification.
- Qualification objection: two V2 inputs must never collapse accidentally. Resolved by including
  all normative resources and static diagnostics in the digest and by the complete-corpus audit.
- Consumer objection: removing architecture from the snapshot may hide it. Resolved by retaining it
  in the context/presentation owner rather than deleting it.
- Native objection: compiler analysis should not rerun for prose. Accepted as a benefit; no native
  fact depends on architecture prose.

## Consensus and decision

Accepted under the TypeSpec owner's explicit direction that V2 must correct and track genuine V1
flaws rather than reproduce them. All four review perspectives have a compatible disposition, no
Kernel safety or soundness objection remains, and the behavior follows the already-ratified
authority separation.
