# V2-REV-021: Qualify causal affected-shard evidence without a permanent historical oracle

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-08-15

Affected requirements: V2-ANA-014, V2-QLF-006

Review perspectives: Codegraph product, TypeScript invalidation, performance methodology, persistence, qualification governance

## Existing decision

EXP-001 required at least five counterbalanced historical/candidate pairs before affected-shard
analysis could graduate. The historical binary also required a qualification-only eager-publication
adapter because it predates commit-late producer acknowledgement.

## Proposed decision

- Independent cold reconstruction remains the exact semantic oracle for every candidate edit.
- The historical binary is used once for isolated cold and algorithmic attribution, not generation
  parity or a permanent regression dependency.
- Incremental graduation requires at least 20 times private-edit speedup on Codegraph and a clean
  Kernel holdout, exact independent-cold equality, affected-scope counters, exact SQLite row deltas,
  and adversarial conservative-fallback proofs.
- A short isolated cold comparison replaces the noisy long matrix. Candidate cold overhead must be
  at most 5 percent or be fixed before graduation.
- The eager-publication adapter, historical matrix runner, and baseline worktree are removed after
  attribution.

## Evidence and motivation

The aborted matrix produced three complete pairs before a candidate cold rebuild took 727,952.98 ms
and the immediately following baseline cold rebuild took 77,273.04 ms. More alternating complete
repository runs would not cheaply distinguish candidate cost from host noise. Every completed edit
was exact against its own cold reconstruction, but the historical and candidate generation
identities differ, confirming that the former is not a valid permanent semantic authority.

The isolated cold-only trial used the same 310-source and 4,736-shard corpus. Historical elapsed time
was 107,811.04 ms; candidate elapsed time was 78,022.31 ms. Candidate projection was 70.76 seconds
versus 99.64 seconds and transport was 1.74 seconds versus 3.09 seconds with effectively unchanged
transaction volume. The candidate is 27.63 percent faster and therefore passes the cold threshold.

Current generated qualification evidence reports:

| Corpus | Private edit | Independent cold | Speedup | Scope | Exact | SQLite payload rows |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | ---: |
| Codegraph | 843.96 ms | 77,065.71 ms | 91.31x | 1 source | yes | 15 for 15 upserts |
| Kernel core | 906.17 ms | 26,036.91 ms | 28.73x | 1 source | yes | 7 for 7 upserts |

The eleven-scenario fixture proves private body and diagnostic edits, computed dependencies, public
shape expansion, import and ambient full fallback, create/delete/rename/configuration universe
rollover, and failed-store-commit replay. Every scenario equals its independent cold result.

## Alternatives

- Finish six pairs despite the outlier. Rejected because repetition was expensive without improving
  causal attribution.
- Remove the 5 percent cold guard. Rejected; the isolated comparison retained and passed it.
- Keep the historical binary in CI. Rejected because its generation preimage and publication
  protocol are obsolete, while cold reconstruction of the candidate is the stronger semantic
  oracle.
- Qualify wall time alone. Rejected; source, shard, fact, allocation, wire, and physical SQLite row
  counters are required to prove that repository-sized work did not merely move layers.

## Compatibility, migration, and gate effects

No public API, fact schema, identity, storage schema, or production protocol changes. V2-ANA-014 is
qualified by `.history/v2/evidence/affected-shard-qualification.json`. The evidence also satisfies
the affected-compiler and immutable-shard portions of V2-QLF-006; that broader row remains
implemented until its portable-pass, heap-limit, and ordinary-query proofs are assembled.

The historical adapter and baseline checkout are deleted. Reproducing candidate evidence requires
only the current binary, Codegraph, a clean Kernel revision, and independent cold reconstruction.

## Objections and dispositions

- Methodology objection: changing a frozen experiment could hide a failure. Resolved by preserving
  all completed samples and their hashes in the diagnostic record and explicitly stating that the
  original distribution threshold was not completed.
- Oracle objection: candidate and historical generations differ. Accepted; historical output is not
  used for semantic parity. Candidate incremental output is compared to candidate cold output.
- Persistence objection: complete immutable membership still writes 4,736 or 2,305 small rows.
  Accepted because changed payload and fact rows are exact deltas and measured commit time is small;
  a delta-manifest schema is not justified by this bottleneck.

## Consensus and decision

Accepted. The architecture graduates on exact semantics plus causal affected-work evidence, while
the obsolete oracle and compatibility seam are retired. This preserves qualification rigor without
turning historical implementation behavior into a permanent constraint on Codegraph.
