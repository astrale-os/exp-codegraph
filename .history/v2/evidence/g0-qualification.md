## Frozen evidence

- `v1-oracle.json`: canonical catalog, normalized verified/unverified transports, 339 source
  payloads, verification profiles and coverage, all direct authoring consumers, public package
  surface, protocol versions, and active V1/legacy inventory.
- `v1-cli.json`: process-level help, usage, focused/empty selection, verification, evidence, output,
  causal diagnostic, and exit-status fixtures.
- `v1-performance.json`: focused, full cold, isolated cache cold/warm, and watcher-readiness time,
  RSS, and cache-size evidence.
- `drift.tsv`: empty governed ledger; no V1/V2 difference is pre-accepted.

their derived transport revisions/generation had entered content hashes, and object construction
order was not canonicalized. The final oracle recomputes envelope identities from canonical,
normalized content and regenerated byte-for-byte on an independent full run.

## Known baseline defects and limits

- Full V1 verification contains 158 pass, 105 fail, 6 idle, and 6 error specifications. Those
  states are frozen observations, not ratified V2 semantics.
- Shell verification cannot install its declared unpublished SDK range; the environmental error
  remains visible rather than being normalized to pass.
- Focused checking approaches 1 GB RSS; the isolated cold full check approaches 1.89 GB RSS; warm
  checking remains about 1.34 GB RSS; and the cache is one opaque 21.2 MiB blob.

Gate 0 qualifies reproducible evidence, not V1 correctness. Any V2 difference must be classified
and justified under `GOVERNANCE.md`; genuine V1 defects are corrected through contract and
regression evidence rather than preserved for parity.
